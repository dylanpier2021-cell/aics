What's the average cost of a commercial cleaning service in Urbana, IL?

Commercial cleaning services in Urbana run between $25 and $50 an hour, or about $0.25 per square foot. The exact price depends on your needs and the size of your property.

From offices and retail spaces to warehouses and factories, professional cleaners can handle everything from routine janitorial work to deep cleaning and specialized services.

Understanding the factors that affect commercial cleaning costs can help you compare quotes, plan your budget, and choose the right cleaning service for your property.

How commercial cleaning is usually priced

Most cleaning companies quote your job in one of three ways, and it helps to know the difference before you start comparing numbers.

  • Hourly pricing: Around Urbana, you can expect roughly $25 to $50 an hour per cleaner, which lines up with what most of the country pays. This model works well when the scope is a little unpredictable, like a one-time cleanup or a space that needs different attention week to week.
  • Per square foot pricing: For standard recurring office cleaning, rates generally fall between $0.10 and $0.25 per square foot. A 2,000 square foot office at $0.15, for example, would run about $300 per visit. This is the easiest way to compare two quotes fairly, since it puts both companies on the same measuring stick.
  • Flat monthly contract: Instead of paying per visit, you pay one set fee for an agreed schedule and scope. Small to midsize offices often land somewhere between $200 and $800 a month for recurring service, while larger buildings or specialized facilities climb from there. Flat contracts tend to give you the most predictable budget, and locking in a recurring plan often earns you a better rate than paying job by job.

None of these numbers are set in stone. They are starting points. The only way to know what your space costs is to have someone walk it and quote the actual work, which is exactly why we come out and look before we give you a price.

Factors affecting the price

Two businesses on the same block can pay very different amounts, and it usually comes down to a handful of things.

  • The size of your space: This is the biggest driver. More square footage means more time, more supplies, and more labor, so the total goes up. That said, very small spaces sometimes cost a little more per square foot, because there is a baseline amount of work in showing up no matter how small the job is.
  • How often you need us: A space cleaned five nights a week is easier to keep up than one cleaned once a month, so the per-visit cost is often lower on a frequent schedule. Less frequent cleaning means more buildup between visits and more work each time.
  • The type of facility: A standard office is not the same as a medical clinic, a restaurant, or a warehouse. Spaces with health codes, heavy foot traffic, or specialized surfaces take more time and stricter methods, and that shows up in the price.
  • The scope of work: Basic janitorial work like dusting, vacuuming, trash removal, and restroom cleaning sits at the lower end. Add in floor stripping and waxing, interior window cleaning, or post-construction cleanup, and the number moves up, because those are separate jobs with their own equipment and time.
  • Condition of the property: A space that has been maintained is quicker to clean than one that has been neglected for months. The first deep clean on a rough space almost always costs more than the regular visits that follow.
  • Supplies and equipment: If you want green-certified products or a job needs specialized machines, that can add to the cost. Most routine work does not, but it is worth asking what is included.

Getting an honest number for your space

The ranges above will give you a ballpark, but a real quote beats a guess every time. Any cleaning company worth hiring should be happy to walk your property, learn what matters to you, and price the work you actually need instead of a generic package.
