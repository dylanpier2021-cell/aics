How much does floor stripping and waxing cost in Champaign County?

Floor stripping and waxing usually cost approximately $0.50 to $3.00 per square foot, with many standard commercial jobs falling somewhere in the middle of that range.

For a 1,000-square-foot floor, that means a rough budget of $500 to $3,000, even though the actual quote in Champaign County depends a lot on the floor's condition, layout, material, and the amount of work needed.

If you're pricing this service for a school, office, healthcare facility, retail space, or other commercial property, the per-square-foot rate is helpful, but it shouldn't be the only number you look at. Here's what actually affects the final cost.

What is included in floor stripping and waxing?

Floor waxing and stripping is more involved than just "putting a fresh layer of finish on a dull floor." The old finish is removed first, the surface is cleaned and prepared, and a new protective finish is applied. The process can include:

  • Removing old wax and built-up finish
  • Deep cleaning and preparing the floor
  • Neutralizing and rinsing the surface
  • Applying sealer where needed
  • Applying multiple coats of floor finish
  • Allowing sufficient drying time between coats

The goal is not just to make the floor look shiny. A properly maintained finish helps protect the underlying flooring from daily foot traffic, dirt, scuffs, and wear.

What makes floor stripping and waxing more expensive?

Two buildings with the exact same square footage can receive very different quotes. That's because the condition and accessibility of the floor can change the amount of labor involved.

1. Condition of the floor

This is among the most important factors. Regular floor maintenance may reduce the amount of time and stripping passes required. However, years of accumulated finish, thick soil, severe scuffing, or neglected sections can make the work considerably more labor-intensive. Expect the price to go toward the higher end of the spectrum if the floor requires significant restoration before completion.

2. Type of flooring

Not all floors are treated the same way. Vinyl composition tile (VCT), with a detachable floor finish, is frequently serviced. Certain surfaces, such as polished concrete, hardwood, natural stone, and luxury vinyl, could need entirely different upkeep techniques. Make sure the contractor has accurately identified the flooring before you pay for a strip-and-wax service.

3. Size and layout

In general, cleaning a broad, open floor takes less time than cleaning multiple small rooms with desks, chairs, storage, and other obstructions. Labor time can be increased by moving furniture, intricate edges, staircases, and hard-to-reach places.

4. Scheduling and access

Finally, the cost may be impacted by additional scheduling needs if the work must be done overnight, on weekends, or near a busy company. Timing is also important for occupied buildings, because it takes time for the floor finish to dry before the area can safely resume regular use.

Why professional floor care can be worth it

Selecting a quote based only on the lowest price can be alluring. However, the proper tools, chemicals, methods, and drying time are needed for stripping and finishing a commercial floor. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported a $17.71 median hourly wage for janitors and building cleaners in May 2025, which helps explain why labor is a major part of professional cleaning costs. Commercial providers also have equipment, transportation, supplies, insurance, and overhead to account for.

There is also a practical indoor-air consideration. The EPA advises adhering to product labeling, avoiding chemical mixing, and boosting ventilation as necessary, because some cleaning solutions can contribute to indoor air pollution.

How often should you strip and wax a commercial floor?

There isn't one schedule that works for every building. Compared to a modest private office, a bustling retail store, school hallway, or healthcare facility can require more frequent floor maintenance. Regular cleaning and lighter maintenance between full stripping jobs can help prevent heavy buildup and extend the life of the finish.

Instead of paying for a full strip right away, find out whether a maintenance treatment could improve the finish's appearance if it is still in good shape but only appears dull.

How to get an accurate floor stripping quote

Before requesting estimates, have a few basic details ready:

  • Approximate square footage
  • Type of flooring
  • Current condition of the floor
  • Whether furniture needs to be moved
  • Preferred service hours
  • Any areas with unusually heavy traffic or buildup

Then compare quotes based on what is actually included. A lower price is not necessarily better if it excludes preparation, additional finish coats, furniture moving, or other necessary work.
