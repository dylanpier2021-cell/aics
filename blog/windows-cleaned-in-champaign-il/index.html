How often should you have your windows cleaned in Champaign County?

You should have your windows cleaned by an expert every 6 months in Champaign County — and more often when construction dust, storms, pollen, or heavy grime cause noticeable buildup.

Clean windows do more than make a home look better. They let in more natural light, help prevent dirt and mineral deposits from becoming tougher to remove, and improve your property's overall appearance. Here's more about it.

How often should you clean your windows?

For most homeowners, twice a year is an ideal starting point. However, the surroundings of your property and the condition of your windows can change that schedule. Have a quick look at the table below to understand it more clearly:

SituationRecommended frequency
Average homeEvery 6 months
High pollen or dustEvery 3 to 4 months
Commercial propertyEvery 1 to 3 months
After constructionAs needed
After severe stormsAs needed

5 factors that affect how often windows need professional cleaning

1. Seasonal pollen

Windows can be covered in pollen in the spring, particularly in the vicinity of houses with gardens, trees, and other plants. Rain can remove part of it, but it can also leave residue and streaks behind. Consider getting your windows cleaned by a professional later in the season if they become dirty or yellowish shortly after spring cleaning.

2. Dust, dirt, and outdoor debris

Compared to properties in more peaceful residential neighborhoods, homes near busy roads, fields, construction sites, or unpaved surfaces may accumulate dirt more quickly. In certain cases, the glass may appear visibly dull if a full year passes between expert cleanings. When buildup occurs quickly, cleaning every three to four months may make more sense.

3. Weather and storms

Water stains, dirt, leaves, and other debris can be left on external glass by rain, wind, and storms. Windows might experience a variety of conditions throughout the year due to Champaign County's fluctuating weather. It's not always necessary to plan a cleaning after each storm. Instead of letting deposits linger for months, it is worthwhile to have the windows cleaned if a storm leaves obvious dirt or water spots.

4. Hard water and mineral deposits

Even while water spots might seem like a small cosmetic problem, they can get harder to get rid of over time. Expert cleaners have the tools and cleaning agents necessary to remove tenacious buildup without a lot of scrubbing. When regular household glass cleaner is no longer yielding clear results, this is especially helpful.

5. The type and location of your windows

The rate at which windows get dirty varies. Compared to sheltered windows, windows facing highways, trees, fields, or construction sites may require more maintenance. It can also be risky and challenging for homeowners to clean their own second-story windows. Sills, screens, exterior and interior glass, and other easily missed spots can all be cleaned by professionals.

Is professional window cleaning better than DIY cleaning?

In between expert visits, homeowners can definitely take care of simple cleaning for windows that are easily accessible. Dust and fingerprints can be managed with a basic routine.

When windows are hard to reach, badly stained, extremely tall, or have collected months' worth of debris, professional cleaning becomes more beneficial. During cleaning, a professional can also spot evident concerns like broken frames, cracked glass, and failing seals that homeowners might miss.

So the two approaches do not necessarily compete. DIY cleaning can handle routine upkeep, while professional window cleaning can provide a deeper seasonal clean and safely reach difficult areas.

When should you schedule window cleaning in Champaign County?

For many homeowners, spring and fall are the easiest times to schedule professional window cleaning. A spring cleaning removes winter grime and prepares the glass for the warmer months. A fall cleaning can clear away summer dust, pollen, and outdoor debris before winter arrives. You should also consider an earlier appointment if:

  • Your windows have visible streaks or water spots.
  • You can see dirt buildup from inside the home.
  • Pollen is covering the exterior glass.
  • You recently completed construction or remodeling.
  • Your windows are difficult or unsafe to reach.
  • You are preparing to sell your home.

Keep your Champaign County windows looking their best

A simple twice-a-year cleaning schedule works very well for a lot of homes, but your surroundings might require more frequent service. If your windows are exposed to dust, heavy pollen, construction debris, or storms, adjusting the schedule can help keep them clearer throughout the year.
